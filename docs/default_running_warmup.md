# Default Running Warm-Up Routine (8--12 Minutes)

Use this before **easy runs, tempo runs, or long runs** to improve
performance and reduce injury risk.

## 1) Easy Walk or Light Jog --- 2 minutes

Start with a relaxed pace to raise body temperature.

-   1 minute brisk walk
-   1 minute light jog

## 2) Ankle + Foot Prep --- 1 minute

Especially useful if you get tight calves, shin splints, or weak ankles.

-   **Ankle circles** --- 10 each direction per foot
-   **Toe raises** --- 15 reps
-   **Heel raises** --- 15 reps

## 3) Dynamic Leg Mobility --- 2 minutes

Wake up hips, hamstrings, and quads.

-   **Leg swings front/back** --- 10 each leg
-   **Leg swings side-to-side** --- 10 each leg
-   **Walking lunges** --- 8 each side

## 4) Hip Activation --- 2 minutes

Important for knee stability and better running form.

-   **Glute bridges** --- 15 reps
-   **Bodyweight squats** --- 15 reps
-   **Lateral band walks** (if band available) --- 10 steps each side

## 5) Running Drills --- 2--3 minutes

Improve coordination and prepare your nervous system.

-   **High knees** --- 20 seconds
-   **Butt kicks** --- 20 seconds
-   **A-skips or marching skips** --- 20 seconds
-   **Straight-leg bounds** --- 20 seconds

## 6) Strides (Optional but Great) --- 2 minutes

Best before speed sessions or races.

-   3 × 40--60 meter relaxed accelerations
-   Start at 60% speed
-   Finish around 85--90%
-   Walk back between reps

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Quick Version (3--5 Minutes)

If you're short on time:

-   1 minute light jog
-   Leg swings
-   Walking lunges
-   High knees
-   2 strides

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Pro Tip

Since you've mentioned **flat feet, weak ankles, and knee discomfort**,
prioritize:

-   ankle circles
-   heel raises
-   glute bridges
-   lateral walks
-   strides

These will help with **foot stability, ankle strength, and knee
tracking**.
